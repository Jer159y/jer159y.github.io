  <p class="home-eyebrow">SOOBIN CHOI / UNIST</p>
  <h2>Keeping long-horizon predictions dynamically honest.</h2>
  <p>I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Mathematical Sciences at UNIST, working on computational dynamics under uncertainty. My current research looks at reservoir computing ensembles for nonlinear systems: how individual members fail during long autonomous rollouts, how they can recover, and how to evaluate predictions by the variability, scales, and statistics they preserve rather than by pointwise error alone.</p>
  <p>Looking ahead, I want to connect these methods to ocean and deep-ocean observation, where measurements are sparse, noisy, and irregular in time, and to uncertainty-aware adaptive sampling.</p>
