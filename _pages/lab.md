### Ocean PDE Lab

Compare the physical assumptions behind ocean PDE models, and integrate 2D Navier–Stokes, rotating shallow-water, and single-layer QG equations directly in your browser.

Change viscosity, rotation, deformation radius, and initial fields, then watch the flow field and its energy/enstrophy budgets evolve.

<small>Idealized, nondimensional models on a periodic domain [0, 2π]². Not an ocean forecast.</small>
